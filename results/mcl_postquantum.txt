==============================================================================
 PART 4B: SENSITIVITY (Growth of a Perturbation)
==============================================================================

 A perturbation of ε in the initial state grows by e^(λ₁×N)
 after N iterations. With λ₁ ≈ 5.78 and N = 10000 (burn-in):
 amplification = e^(5.78 × 10000) = 10^25102

 Empirical verification (ε = 10⁻¹⁵ to 10⁻³):
 ε Sat iters Amplification bits Status
 ----------------------------------------------------------
 1e-15   6 51.9 SATURATED
 1e-12   5 41.9 SATURATED
 1e-09   4 32.0 SATURATED
 1e-06   2 22.0 SATURATED
 1e-03   1 12.0 SATURATED

 Theoretical (unbounded system): e^(5.78 × 10000) = 10^25102
 NOTE: On the torus T² = [0,2π)², divergence SATURATES at ≈ π.
 The 10^25102 figure is a linear extrapolation of the Lyapunov
 exponent for an unbounded system. Empirically, small ε saturates
 within ~50 iterations (see table above). Any perturbation, no matter
 how small, reaches full decorrelation — the RATE is exponential,
 the AMOUNT is bounded by π. This is sensitivity of the iteration;
 a finite-precision realization is still eventually periodic.
 [PASS] Sensitivity                    exponential divergence confirmed

 A pair (p, q) alone is below the 64-bit level at every range, up to and

 The 256-bit keyed configuration (keyed_q30_PQ/) carries the key in

 That is a generic figure. It holds only in the absence of a structural
 attack, which this program does not examine.

==============================================================================
 PART 6: WHAT THESE DIAGNOSTICS DO NOT SHOW
==============================================================================

 1. No quantum algorithm is run, simulated or bounded by this program.
 2. A spectral test on 65536 bytes and a period scan up to 20000 say nothing
    about longer periods. Every finite-precision realization is
    eventually periodic; cycle lengths are measured in T4_CycleStructure/.
 3. The construction has no public key, no modulus to factor and no
    discrete logarithm to take. That is read off the construction, not
    measured here, and it says nothing about other period-finding
    attacks (point 4).
 4. The integer map has an exact translation symmetry of its state
    (T4_CycleStructure/). Absence of structure is therefore not claimed.
 5. Whether a quantum algorithm shortens the sequential depth of the
    iteration is open.
 6. No proof, no reduction to a standard assumption, and no independent
    cryptanalysis exist for the map.
