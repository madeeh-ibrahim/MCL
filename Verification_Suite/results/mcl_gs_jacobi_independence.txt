==============================================================================
  GS vs JACOBI INDEPENDENCE -- distance correlation (n = 5000)
==============================================================================

  Pearson r tests only LINEAR independence. Distance
  correlation is 0 iff FULL (linear + nonlinear) statistical
  independence. dCor is upward-biased at finite n, so we
  calibrate against a permutation null and report z.
  Trajectories come from the engine's own mcl_iterate_raw
  (Gauss-Seidel) and mcl_iterate_jacobi -- bit-identical to
  production, no port drift.

  (A) GS vs Jacobi (TEST SUBJECT), n=5000:
      theta1: dCor = 0.0312, null 0.0213 +- 0.0048, z = +2.06
      theta2: dCor = 0.0156, null 0.0207 +- 0.0049, z = -1.04
      (theta2 included because the GS/Jacobi difference is in
      t2's update; a dependence could surface there.)

==============================================================================
  SIZE-STABILITY SWEEP -- does z grow with n? (hidden-dependence test)

  keeps z oscillating around 0. This is the decisive check.

==============================================================================
  DETECTION POWER -- weakest dependence the test can see at this n
==============================================================================

  We inject a KNOWN weak dependence of strength eps into the
  REAL chaotic series: a Jacobi partner contaminated as
  jc = (1-eps)*jx + eps*gx, on the correct distribution (not a
  uniform surrogate). The smallest eps with z > 3 is the
  sensitivity floor: any GS-vs-Jacobi dependence weaker than
  this would be invisible, so the floor bounds what the
  'independent' verdict above actually rules out.

  (measured on the real series at n=3000; floor improves ~2x
  per 4x increase in n, since z ~ eps*sqrt(n))

  eps        z

  0.05       +6.78    <-- detected
  0.10       +12.76   <-- detected

  Sensitivity floor (smallest detected eps): ~5%
