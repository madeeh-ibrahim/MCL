==============================================================================
  TEST 2: CHANNELS INDEPENDENT (inter-oscillator byte MI, N=3)
==============================================================================

  Per-oscillator byte MI should be ~ 0 (independent).
  NEGATIVE control (synchronized Kuramoto net) MUST be >> 0.
  (This is per-oscillator/multi-receiver channel independence,
  not the merged gen_byte randomness -- that is PractRand's job.)

  REAL (triple 0):  MI(1;2)=-0.000009  MI(1;3)=-0.000014  MI(2;3)=-0.000017
  SYNC control:     MI(1;2)=+3.999974  MI(1;3)=+3.961449  MI(2;3)=+3.961449

  Real-engine max |MI| = 0.000017 (independent if < 0.01)
  Sync-control min MI  = 3.9614 (detector valid if > 1.0)

==============================================================================
  VERDICT: DOES THE MCL TOPOLOGY GENERALIZE TO N >= 3?

  T1 PASS: lambda_1 > 0 for all tested N=3 and N=4
           configurations -- chaos persists with the
           higher-order topology.
  T2 PASS: real-engine inter-channel MI ~ 0 (independent),
           and the synchronized control MI is high
           (3.96 bits), so the estimator is valid. The

  OVERALL: GENERALIZES -- both chaos and channel independence hold
